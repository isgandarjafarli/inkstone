# Support

Thanks for using Inkstone. Here is how to get help.

## Common questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How do I put a word on my lock screen? | Press and hold the lock screen, tap **Customize → Lock Screen**, tap the box under the clock and choose **Inkstone**. |
| How do I add home screen widgets? | Press and hold an empty area, tap **Edit → Add Widget** and search for **Inkstone**. |
| How do I put a word on my watch face? | Touch and hold the watch face, tap **Edit**, swipe to the complications, tap a spot and choose **Inkstone**. |
| The watch only shows HSK 1 words | Open Inkstone on your iPhone once, so it sends your words to the watch. |
| How do I change my word list or level? | Open the **Progress** tab and tap the gear to open **Settings**. |
| Can I move my progress to a new phone? | Yes. In **Settings**, tap **Export progress** and save the file. On the new phone, tap **Import progress**. |
| The voice sounds robotic | Download an enhanced Chinese voice in **Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese** on your iPhone. |
| Does the app need the internet? | No. Everything works offline. |

## Still stuck?

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/isgandarjafarli/inkstone/issues/new) and describe what happened. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and app version (shown in **Settings → About and data sources**).

Our [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) explains how your data is handled: the app collects none.
