# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

Inkstone is an app for learning Chinese words on iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy covers the app, its widgets and its Apple Watch app.

## The short version

**Inkstone does not collect any data.** Nothing you do in the app leaves your devices, except through Apple features that you choose to use, such as iCloud backup.

## What stays on your devices

| What | Where it is kept | Who can see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your study progress: words learned, review history, streak | On your iPhone, shared with the app's own widgets | Only you |
| Your settings: word list, level, tone colours, reminder time | On your iPhone | Only you |
| Today's words and review answers on the watch | On your Apple Watch, sent to and from your own iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity | Only you |
| A progress file you export | Wherever you choose to save or send it | Whoever you share it with |

## What the app does not do

- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It makes no network requests. Words, sentences, stroke data and the font are built into the app.
- It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and no third-party code that does.
- It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

## Features that use Apple services

| Feature | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Pronunciation** | The app uses the Chinese voice built into iOS and watchOS. Speech is made on your device. |
| **Daily reminder** | If you turn it on, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server. |
| **Backups** | Your iPhone may include the app's data in your iCloud or computer backups, as with any app. Apple's privacy policy covers those. |

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone and Apple Watch. You can also use **Settings → Reset all progress** in the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
